How AquaSentinel Works

Your AI Marine Ranger — Powered by Vision AI & Geo-Intelligence.

1
📤

Upload Your Marine Image

Begin your marine discovery journey by uploading a clear image of an ocean species. AquaSentinel's advanced Vision AI system prepares your image for intelligent analysis, ensuring optimal detection accuracy.

  • Select any ocean species photograph from your device
  • Supports JPG, PNG, and other common image formats
  • High-quality images yield more accurate results
  • Instant upload with secure processing
💡 Tip: Clear, well-lit images work best for species identification
2
🌍

Detect Your Location

AquaSentinel automatically detects your geographical coordinates using your browser's location services. This data is then reverse-geocoded through OpenStreetMap Nominatim to identify the nearest ocean or sea region.

  • Browser fetches precise latitude and longitude
  • Location reverse-geocoded using OpenStreetMap Nominatim
  • Identifies the closest ocean, sea, or marine region
  • Privacy-focused: location data used only for identification
📍 Example: "Region Identified: Arabian Sea, India"
3
🤖

AI Identifies the Species

The magic happens here. Your image and regional data are processed by Gemini 2.5 Flash, Google's cutting-edge Vision AI model. The system recognizes the species, validates its habitat match, assesses rarity, and generates a comprehensive marine intelligence report.

  • Image analyzed by Gemini 2.5 Flash Vision AI
  • Cross-references species with regional habitat data
  • Evaluates rarity and conservation status
  • Generates detailed report with species info, threats, and fun facts
🔬 Powered by: Gemini 2.5 Flash AI + Geo-Intelligence

⚠️ Responsible AI Usage

While our AI is highly advanced, it may not be 100% accurate in every case. Species identification depends on image quality and available data.
